About Frostfall: Last Fire

In a world locked beneath endless snow, one furnace is all that keeps humanity alive. Gather resources, grow your city, recruit heroes and work with real players across a shared world map. Alliances can chat, help each other build, fight together and compete for the Eternal Hearth Citadel.

Version 1.5.1 introduces a redesigned chat, a new alliance screen, map search, a travelling messenger system and events that continue while players are away.
